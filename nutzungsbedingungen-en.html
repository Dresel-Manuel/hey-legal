2. What the app does

"hey!" gets people talking through question cards. The deck you take along shows its sending card to people near you. You see the cards of others, send a request if you are interested, and if the other person says yes, you meet at a table and play there together with your decks.

The app is in a test and pilot phase. Features may change, fail or be discontinued.

For the live features the app creates an anonymous account (no email or phone number). Details are in the privacy policy.

3. Minimum age

4a. Notices, moderation and point of contact (Digital Services Act)

Reporting and blocking in the app: you report a sending card with "Report card" while swiping. You report or block a person you had a request or a meeting with on the request, during the meeting, after a round, or later under Profile → Encounters (for 30 days). Blocking works both ways: you no longer see each other on the map, cannot send each other requests, and open requests and meetings between you are cancelled. The blocked or reported person is not told who blocked or reported them. You can lift a block under Profile → Encounters.

Reporting illegal content: if you see content in hey! that you consider illegal (e.g. a question, a deck name or a table name), email Dresel-Manuel@web.de with the subject "hey! report". Please state: what exactly you are reporting (the text, the deck name, roughly when and where you saw it), why you think the content is illegal, your name and email address, and a statement that your information is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge (Art. 16 DSA). If you report sexual abuse of children, you do not have to give your name. We confirm receipt and tell you our decision.

How we review: we review notices and breaches of section 4 by hand and promptly; we do not use automated filters or content detection. Possible measures are removing content, hiding a table or suspending an account. A suspended account is no longer shown to others and cannot send requests. The only automatic step: if three different people have reported the same account, it is provisionally no longer shown and cannot send requests until we have reviewed it. A human always decides on a suspension. We act diligently, objectively and proportionately and take into account the fundamental rights of everyone involved, in particular freedom of expression.

Reasons and objection: if we restrict your content or account, we tell you why (Art. 17 DSA). If your account is provisionally hidden or suspended, the app shows this under Profile → Encounters with a short reason. Because accounts in hey! are anonymous, we can only write to you beyond that if you have given us a way to contact you. You can object to the decision by email; we will then review it again. You can always go to court.

Point of contact: our single point of contact for users and for authorities of the Member States, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Art. 11 and 12 DSA) is Dresel-Manuel@web.de. Languages: German and English.

Danger to life or safety: if we become aware of information giving rise to a suspicion of a criminal offence involving a threat to the life or safety of persons, we inform the competent authorities (Art. 18 DSA). In an acute emergency, please call 112 yourself.

5. Meetings and tables

  • Official tables are set by us or by organisers at public places, including inside an event's area.
  • Own tables are set by users themselves. We do not check them. Only suggest and accept public, busy places, never a private home.
  • A meeting is voluntary. You can cancel at any time ("I'm not coming after all"), at short notice and without giving a reason.
  • We only bring people into contact. We do not know the other users and are not responsible for their behaviour.

6. Safety feature: emergency button

Holding the emergency button for 4 seconds opens your SMS app with a pre-written message to your emergency contacts.

  • The emergency feature is not a substitute for the emergency number (112/110). In an acute emergency always call the emergency number first.
  • The app does not send the message automatically. You must confirm sending yourself.
  • Delivery depends on your mobile provider, network reception and the recipients' devices.
  • You are responsible for your own safety when meeting people.

Recommendations: meet at public, busy places. Tell someone you trust where you are. Set up your emergency contacts beforehand. Leave if you feel uncomfortable.
