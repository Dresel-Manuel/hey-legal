2. What the app does

„hey!“ is a social discovery app that encourages people to meet in person through question-card games. The app runs entirely locally on your device. There is no server, no backend and no cloud connection.

5. Safety feature: emergency button

The app contains an emergency feature. Holding the emergency button for 4 seconds opens your default SMS app with a pre-written message to the emergency contacts you have stored.

Important notes on the safety feature:

  • The emergency feature is NOT a substitute for the emergency services (112/110). In an acute emergency, always call the emergency number first.
  • The app does NOT send the message automatically. You have to confirm sending it yourself in your SMS app.
  • The developer gives no guarantee that the SMS will be delivered.
  • If you cannot operate your phone, no message will be sent.
  • The safety feature is an additional aid, not a guarantee of your safety.
  • You remain responsible for your own safety when meeting people in person.

Recommendations for meeting safely:

  • Meet in public, busy places.
  • Tell someone you trust where and when you are meeting.
  • Set up your emergency contacts in the app before you use it to meet someone.
  • Leave a situation immediately if you feel uncomfortable.
