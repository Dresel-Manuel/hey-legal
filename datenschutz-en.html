2. Core principle: all data stays on your device

The „hey!“ app follows a strict local-first approach. This means:

  • There is no server, no backend and no cloud connection.
  • All data is stored exclusively on your device.
  • No data is transmitted to the developer, to third parties or to an external server.
  • There are no user accounts, no registration and no server-side authentication.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising services are used.

The only exception is the emergency SMS feature described in section 5, which is triggered solely by you.

3. What data does the app store locally?

The app stores the following data exclusively on your device (in local storage via SharedPreferences):

User profile:

  • the display name you chose

Emergency contacts:

  • names and phone numbers of the emergency contacts you selected

Settings:

  • safety settings (auto check-in, silent alarm)
  • Bluetooth settings

Consent records:

  • age confirmation (18+) with timestamp
  • acceptance of the terms of use with timestamp
  • acknowledgement of the safety notices with timestamp
  • consent version

None of this data leaves your device. There is no synchronisation, no backup to external servers and no transmission to the developer.

4. Legal basis for processing

The local storage of the data listed in section 3 is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract). The data is technically necessary to provide the app's core functions.

The age confirmation and consent records are stored on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR (legal obligation).

5. Emergency SMS feature

The app contains an emergency feature. If you hold the emergency button for 4 seconds, the app opens your SMS application with a pre-written message to the emergency contacts you have stored.

Important to understand:

  • The message is NOT sent automatically. The app merely opens your default SMS app with the text and recipient pre-filled. You have to confirm sending it yourself by tapping „send“.
  • The SMS is sent via your mobile network operator, not via the app or a server of the developer.
  • The app has no access to the actual sending and cannot track it.
  • The data transmitted in the process (the recipient's phone number, the message text) is subject to the privacy terms of your mobile network operator.

6. App permissions

Contacts: so that you can select your emergency contacts from your address book. The selected contact details are stored locally only. Your full address book is not read, copied or transmitted.

Bluetooth: for detecting other app users nearby. Bluetooth communication takes place directly between devices (peer-to-peer). No location data is collected and no data is sent to any server.

Each permission is requested individually. You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's system settings.

7. No transfer to third parties

No personal data is transferred to third parties.

8. No transfer to third countries

As no data leaves the device, no transfer to third countries takes place.

9. Storage period and deletion

All locally stored data remains on your device until you do one of the following:

  • withdraw your consent in the app (under settings)
  • clear the app's data in your device's system settings
  • uninstall the app

10. Your rights

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR): all stored data can be viewed directly in the app.
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): you can change all data directly in the app.
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): by uninstalling the app or withdrawing your consent.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Withdrawal of consent given (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

Complaints: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information), Alt-Moabit 59-61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, mailbox@datenschutz-berlin.de

11. Data security

The locally stored data is protected by the security mechanisms of your operating system (Android/iOS).

12. Minimum age

Use of the app requires a minimum age of 18.

13. Changes

In the event of substantial changes, the consent version in the app is increased. You will be asked to acknowledge the changes the next time you start the app.
